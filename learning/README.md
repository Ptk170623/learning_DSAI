## How a part is studied

1. One concept at a time, in chat: a plain definition, a tiny example worked by hand, then the same idea on the project's own data.
2. The concept is explained back in your own words and corrected, and a check question is answered, before moving on.
3. When the whole part is understood, the status in the table above and in the part file is updated, the review dates are set, and a way to apply the part in this project is chosen and written in the part file.
4. Reviews are done on the scheduled dates and marked as done.

The books are in the repository root.
