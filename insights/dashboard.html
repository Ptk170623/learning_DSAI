Monthly revenue

Uses the segment chosen on "A typical sale". The dashed lines show the median month and a typical swing (one robust spread, the median absolute deviation scaled to a standard deviation) around it.

Do fixed bands fit? Last 24 months against the 12 months before each

Each bar is the month's revenue against the average of the 12 previous months. The app's NEUTRAL band is a fixed ±10%. A ring marks the months that are unusual judged against the spread of the history itself (more than 2 robust spreads from the median).

Which segments swing the most

Monthly revenue over the last 24 months: coefficient of variation (standard deviation as a share of the mean). Top 15 blocks by revenue.

Seasonal rhythm

Each calendar month against the yearly trend (100% = on trend), typical value over the years; the line shows the lowest to highest year.

Days since the last visit

Typical wait by group

Median days since the last visit; the line runs from the first to the third quartile. Groups with at least 40 doctors with a recorded visit. The dashed line is 90 days.

Churn by payment type and sale type

Share of patient lines flagged as churn in the data, for sales since 2024 (lines flagged as a suspicious dose are left out). Older sales are left out because the flag is rarely set for them. A group at 0% has no churn flagged in the data.
